Features

Everything that matters between two sets.

Less typing, more lifting: Pulse Strength takes care of the paperwork so you can stay with the iron.

Templates & history

Start from your own workout templates and see in your history how weights and reps develop.

Active workout

A set table with its own keypad, supersets and undo – so logging is quick and typos don't matter.

Rest timer

The timer keeps running on the Lock Screen. When the rest is over you get a nudge – a haptic tap on your Watch.

Personal records

Pulse detects personal bests automatically and shows them right in the workout and in the summary.

Exercises with form demos

25 exercises in the library, each with two images showing clean form.

Body weight & Apple Health

Log your body weight or sync it with Apple Health – with a reminder to weigh in regularly.

Everywhere you train

iPhone, Apple Watch, Lock Screen.

Pulse Strength runs wherever you are looking – no need to unlock your phone between sets.

Apple Watch

The Watch mirrors your workout and records your heart rate while you train.

Live Activity

On the Lock Screen you see your set and rest timer and can log sets straight from there.

Dynamic Island

On iPhones with a Dynamic Island (iPhone 14 Pro and newer) the timer is always in view.

Requires iOS 17 / watchOS 10 or newer. The Dynamic Island needs an iPhone 14 Pro or newer.

Privacy

Your data belongs to you.

Pulse is built so that as little as possible leaves your device – and what does leave it stays in the EU.

Health data stays local

Heart rate, HRV, sleep and your nutrition logs from Apple Health never leave your iPhone.

Encrypted in the EU

Once you are signed in, your workout history and profile are stored encrypted on a server in Ireland – so nothing is lost when you switch phones.

AI only if you want it

AI coaching is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, selected details go to providers in France and the Netherlands – never to the US.

View, export, delete

Any time. Deleting your account removes all server data immediately. Nothing is ever sold.

For sign-in, your email address is passed to the authentication service Clerk (USA, with legal safeguards). Full details are in the privacy policy.

Pulse Strength is a focused gym and strength tracker. Pick a template or start empty, log every set in seconds, rest on time and watch your lifts go up.

LOG FAST
• A clear set table with your previous results next to every set
• Number keypad with quick − / + steppers and a plate calculator for barbell lifts
• Warm-up, drop and to-failure sets, reps in reserve, notes and supersets
• Undo for a removed exercise or a deleted set

REST ON TIME
• The rest timer starts automatically after each set; add or remove 15 seconds or skip it
• Control the workout from the lock screen: the notification shows your next set with Log set and rest buttons
• A sound and vibration tell you when the rest is over, even with the app in the background

BEAT YOUR BESTS
• Personal records are flagged the moment you set them, based on your estimated one-rep max
• A coach tip shows the weight that would beat your best on your last set
• Exercise history with an estimated-1RM trend chart, best sets and records
• A summary after every workout: volume, sets per muscle group and new PRs

BUILT-IN EXERCISE LIBRARY
• 36 exercises with how-to cues and form photos, plus your own custom exercises
• Muscle recovery shows which muscle groups are ready, based on your own training

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet permission
• Your workouts stay on your phone (and in your Android backup, if you use one)
• Optional Health Connect: save finished workouts as strength-training sessions. Pulse Strength only writes to Health Connect and never reads your health data

Weights are in kilograms.

Privacy Policy – Pulse Strength for Android

Effective Date: October 16, 2026
Language: English | Deutsch
See also: Privacy Policy of the Pulse app (iPhone) · Impressum

In short

Pulse Strength for Android is a workout log. It works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no internet access (the app does not even request the Android permission needed to connect to a network). Everything you log stays on your phone (and on your paired watch, if you use the optional watch app, see 5a). We do not receive, collect, sell or share any of it.

3. Notifications

While a workout runs, the app shows a notification with the current set, a timer and buttons, and it can sound when a rest is over. These notifications are created on your phone from your workout data and are not sent anywhere. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission when you start your first workout; you can change this at any time in the system settings.

4. Android backup and moving to a new phone

If you use Android's backup, the system (Google) may back up the app's workout data to your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone, or copy it directly when you transfer to a new phone. This is a function of Android and is governed by Google's terms and your Google account settings; we have no access to these backups. You can turn app backup off in your phone's system settings. The workout in progress and the app's settings are not included.

5. Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on "Save workouts to Health Connect" in the app's settings and grant the permission, the app saves each finished workout to Health Connect on your phone as a strength-training session: start time, end time, the workout's name and a note with the number of sets and the total weight lifted. The app only writes to Health Connect; it never reads any health data. This feature is off until you turn it on. You can turn it off in the app or revoke the permission and delete the saved sessions in Health Connect at any time. What Health Connect and other apps do with data stored there is governed by Health Connect and those apps, not by Pulse Strength. The app's use of information received from Health Connect and of the data it writes there is limited to providing this feature; it is never used for advertising and never sold.

5a. Wear OS watch app (optional)

If you install the optional Pulse Strength watch app on a Wear OS watch that is paired with your phone, the phone app sends the watch a summary of your workout data so that the watch can show it: the name of your next workout, the workouts you did this week and this month, your last workout, your latest personal best, the trend of a lift and, while a workout runs, the current exercise and set, the weight and repetitions, and the rest timer. When you tap a button on the watch (for example Log set, add or skip rest, pause, finish or start a workout), the watch sends that command back to the phone. This transfer uses the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services, which moves data between a phone and the watch paired with it and may relay it when the two devices are not directly connected; how it travels (for example over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi) is governed by Google's terms and by the settings of your Google account and your watch. The app does not send the data to us or to anyone else. The watch app keeps the latest summary in its private storage on the watch and, while a workout runs, shows a notification (the ongoing-activity chip on the watch face). Without the watch app nothing is sent. Uninstalling the watch app deletes the summary on the watch.

6. Sharing, selling, advertising

We do not share your data with anyone, we do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not transfer it to other countries – because it never reaches us.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your phone. You can also open the phone's system settings, choose Apps, then Pulse Strength, then Storage, and clear the app's storage. Workout sessions saved to Health Connect are deleted in Health Connect. A backup in your Google account is managed in your Google account's backup settings.

8. Children

The app is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it processes no children's data.

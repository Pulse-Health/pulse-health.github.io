4. Apple HealthKit

Data Collected: Heart rate, workout data, fitness metrics, sleep data, plus your date of birth and biological sex, from Apple Watch and iPhone. Your date of birth is used on your device only (to derive an age range) and is never sent to the AI provider.

Storage: This data remains on your device and your private iCloud. Pulse only reads it with your explicit permission.

Important: HealthKit data cannot be shared with third parties without your permission. We never sell this data.

4a. Apple Health Records (optional)

If you enable it, Pulse can import your Apple Health clinical records (allergies, conditions, immunizations, lab results, medications, procedures, vital signs) so you can view them inside the app.

  • Opt-in: off by default; imported only after you explicitly enable it.
  • Storage: on your device only — not stored on Supabase.
  • Never sent to the AI provider: clinical records are excluded from AI coaching.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • Retention: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

7. AI Coaching (Eden AI → Nebius, EU-internal)

Processing pipeline: AI-coaching requests pass from a Supabase Edge Function in the EU to Eden AI (Eden AI SAS, France, EU), an AI-API aggregator, which forwards them to the underlying language model Nebius / Qwen3-235B (Nebius Group N.V., Netherlands, EU). The entire AI-coaching pipeline runs inside the EU, so there is no third-country transfer for coaching. OpenAI is no longer used for AI coaching.

Legal basis: Before AI Coaching is activated, you give explicit consent during onboarding to the processing of your health data for AI coaching purposes (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Because the pipeline is EU-internal, no separate consent to a third-country transfer is required. AI Coaching is not available unless this consent is given. You may withdraw it at any time by disabling AI Coaching in app settings; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Data sent to the AI provider per coaching request:

  • Your name (pseudonymized) — Your preferred name is not sent in plain text. Instead, Pulse uses a placeholder (%name%) that the AI model includes in its responses. The placeholder is replaced with your real name only on your device before the response is displayed to you. The AI provider never learns your actual name.
  • Your age range (e.g. 20–29) — never your exact age or date of birth
  • Your biological sex (from Apple Health or manual entry)
  • Your height and weight (from Apple Health or manual entry)
  • Your coaching question (the message you typed)
  • Relevant memory snippets — up to 5,000 characters of saved coaching context and notes stored in the app (also using the pseudonymized name placeholder)
  • Health analysis summaries — computed scores and trend analyses produced on your device (not raw sensor readings such as individual HRV samples)

Safeguards:

  • Name pseudonymization: Your name is not included in data sent to the AI provider, which significantly reduces the risk of identification — though other data (biological sex, height, weight, and your coaching question) is still sent.
  • Data minimization: We do not send raw HealthKit sensor readings (e.g. individual heart-rate measurements) or your complete chat history.
  • Encryption in transit: All data is encrypted via TLS 1.3 during transmission.
  • EU relay: Requests pass through a Supabase Edge Function in the EU, serving as an intermediary layer before reaching Eden AI (France) and Nebius (Netherlands).

What is NOT sent to the AI provider: Your email address, authentication credentials, raw HealthKit sensor readings (e.g. individual heart-rate measurements), or your complete chat history (the AI provider receives only a summary of your coaching history).

Location: 🇪🇺 European Union (Eden AI in France; Nebius in the Netherlands)

Retention: Retention of coaching-request data is governed by the Eden AI and Nebius data-processing terms. We are finalizing the applicable data-processing agreement and will update this page once the retention period is confirmed.

Protection & Transfer Mechanism: Because the AI-coaching pipeline is EU-internal (Eden AI in France forwarding to Nebius in the Netherlands), no third-country transfer arises for coaching. An Eden AI data-processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) and a Nebius due-diligence review are being finalized before broad rollout (Eden AI DPA + Nebius due-diligence pending).

Opt-out: You can disable AI Coaching in the app settings at any time. All fitness, nutrition, and sleep features work offline without AI Coaching.

8. Food Database Lookups (Nutrition Feature)

When you search for foods or scan a barcode in the nutrition feature, Pulse sends the search term or barcode number to one or both of the following public food databases to retrieve nutritional information. No health data, account data, or personal identifiers are included in these requests — only the food name or barcode number and your device's IP address (inherent to any internet request).

Results are cached locally on your device; search terms are never stored on Pulse's servers. Photos taken during barcode scanning are processed entirely on-device and are never transmitted anywhere.

4. Apple HealthKit

Data Collected: Heart rate, workout data, fitness metrics, sleep data, plus your date of birth and biological sex, from Apple Watch and iPhone. Your date of birth is used on your device only (to derive an age range) and is never sent to the AI provider. If you turn on the body weight sync in the Strength settings, your body weight is also read from and written to Apple Health (Section 4d).

Storage: This data remains on your device and your private iCloud. Pulse only reads it with your explicit permission.

Important: HealthKit data cannot be shared with third parties without your permission. We never sell this data.

4a. Apple Health Records (optional)

If you enable it, Pulse can import your Apple Health clinical records (allergies, conditions, immunizations, lab results, medications, procedures, vital signs) so you can view them inside the app.

  • Opt-in: off by default; imported only after you explicitly enable it.
  • Storage: on your device only — not stored on Supabase.
  • Never sent to the AI provider: clinical records are excluded from AI coaching.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • Retention: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

4b. Caffeine, Alcohol & Medication Log (optional, on-device only)

If you use it, Pulse lets you record what you consumed and when — caffeine, alcohol, water, and medication you have taken — and shows you whether those amounts move together with your own health metrics (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and so on).

  • Opt-in: nothing is recorded unless you enter it yourself.
  • Storage: on your device only. Individual entries — what you took, how much, and at what time — are never sent anywhere. Medication entries are held in a separately protected file, excluded from backups, and erased when you sign out.
  • What can reach the AI coach: only a daily total for caffeine, alcohol and water, plus any finished statistical result. Medication is not part of that daily summary, and individual entries (substance, dose, time) never reach the coach. Only if you give separate consent for medication does the coach receive the drug class you declared (for example beta blocker, SSRI/SNRI or thyroid hormone — never the substance, brand, dose or time) and any finished aggregate finding about it.
  • Never written to Apple Health: alcohol is read-only, and medication is never written back at all. Caffeine and water are only synced if you switch that on.
  • Descriptive only: Pulse reports whether two things moved together, always with the number of days behind it. That is not proof that one caused the other, and Pulse says so. It gives no dosing, treatment or consumption advice.
  • Honest refusals: below 14 matching days — or 5 days per group — Pulse reports no figure at all, rather than a weak number with a warning attached.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); medication under its own separate consent.
  • Retention: kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

4c. AI-Generated Training & Meal Plans (Premium, optional)

As a Premium feature, Pulse can create a training programme, a workout template or a meal plan for you with the help of AI, based on the settings you choose.

  • Opt-in: a plan is only generated when you request one, and only while AI Coaching is enabled — the same consent and the same on/off switch apply as for the AI coach (Section 7).
  • What is sent to the AI provider: the settings you choose — goal, experience level, training days per week and duration, equipment, sport, body areas you want to avoid loading (for example knees or lower back) and whether you are 18 or older — and, if you entered them during onboarding, your illness notes and physical limitations (free text, each up to 500 characters; they are passed on so that the generator avoids movements that conflict with them) — plus the plan you are currently revising, if any, and any short text you add yourself (for meal plans: dietary preferences, calorie and protein targets, meals per day). The AI provider does not receive your email address or raw Apple Health data.
  • Where it is processed: in the same EU-internal pipeline as AI Coaching (Section 7) — no third-country transfer.
  • Storage: the plan that is created is saved on your device. Pulse's own catalogue of ready-made plan templates is stored on Supabase (EU) and contains no personal data.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), because plan settings such as body areas to avoid, and any illness notes or physical limitations you entered during onboarding, can reveal health information.
  • Not medical advice: plans are general suggestions; see Sections 7 and 8 of our Terms of Service.

4d. Body Weight (Strength, optional)

If you use the Strength feature, Pulse lets you record your body weight over time and can remind you to update it. Your weigh-ins are used to show your history and, once you have logged a weight, two estimates on an exercise’s records page: your strength relative to your body weight (barbell lifts) and an estimated load for bodyweight exercises such as push-ups, dips and pull-ups. Both are calculated on your device.

  • Opt-in: nothing is recorded unless you enter it yourself (in the Strength settings, or with the “Log weight” action of a reminder notification) or you turn on the Apple Health sync described below.
  • What is stored: the date, time and weight (kg) of each weigh-in, whether it was entered in Pulse or came from Apple Health, and your reminder settings (on/off, how often, time of day).
  • Storage: on your device only. Weigh-ins are never sent to our servers (Supabase) or to any other recipient, and they are not part of any request to the AI coach. They are separate from the height and weight in your profile (Section 7). Like other app data, they may be included in a backup of your iPhone that Apple makes (iCloud or computer backup); we have no access to it.
  • Reminder: a local notification scheduled by your iPhone — no server is involved. Nothing is scheduled unless you turn the reminder on in the Strength settings. It uses the app’s notification permission (the same one as the rest-timer alert), which you can change in the iOS Settings at any time. While the reminder is on, a weigh-in that is due is also shown as a card inside the app.
  • Apple Health (optional, off by default): if you turn on “Sync with Apple Health” in the Strength settings, Pulse asks for your permission to read and to write your body weight in Apple Health. While it is on, Pulse saves each weight you enter to Apple Health, removes that entry from Apple Health again when you delete it in Pulse, and adds the body weights that Apple Health already has (for example from a smart scale or another app) to your history in Pulse. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the iOS Settings (Health → Data Access & Devices) or turn the sync off in Pulse.
  • Not shared: your body weight is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future by turning off the reminder or the sync, deleting your weigh-ins or uninstalling the app.
  • Retention: on your device until you delete the weigh-in or uninstall the app. Weigh-ins you entered yourself can be deleted in the Strength settings. A weight that was copied from Apple Health is read-only in Pulse and cannot be deleted there individually; it is removed when you uninstall the app and stays in Apple Health, where you manage it.

5. Supabase Data Storage

Stores: Your user profile and workout history. If you use AI Coaching, Supabase also stores your chat messages, the health-analysis summaries computed on your device, and the health context you enter during onboarding (e.g. illness notes and physical limitations). These are special-category health data, processed on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Nutrition logs are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to Supabase.

Location: 🇪🇺 European Union (EU servers, Frankfurt, Germany)

Protection: Your data is fully protected by GDPR. Supabase servers are hosted in the EU, so there are no data transfer concerns.

Data Processing Agreement: Yes, Supabase is DPA-compliant

Retention: Your data is stored on Supabase until you delete your account. When you delete your account, all data linked to your account is permanently deleted, including your AI usage log and AI error log (Section 5a). Anonymous entries that are not linked to any account (the anonymous question sample and the anonymous plan statistics, Section 5a) cannot be assigned to you and therefore remain. Data already passed to the AI providers follows their retention terms (Section 7).

5a. Operational Logs & Anonymous Statistics

To keep the AI features reliable, within budget and protected from abuse, the backend records limited technical information. The AI usage log and the AI error log contain no message text, health values or plan contents. The anonymous question sample (below) is the only exception: it stores the text of coaching questions, without any link to your account.

  • AI usage log (linked to your account): time, feature, AI model used, whether reasoning mode was used, and token counts. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (providing the service, cost control, abuse prevention). Deleted with your account.
  • AI error log (linked to your account): time, feature, model, app version, error type, HTTP status, a truncated technical error message and duration for failed AI requests — never anything you typed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (keeping the service working). Deleted with your account.
  • Anonymous question sample (not linked to you): to understand what people ask the coach and to improve it, the text of coaching questions is stored with the date, language and the tools the coach used — but without any user ID, session ID or time of day. Before storing, email addresses, links, dates, phone numbers and long ID numbers are replaced automatically with placeholders. This cannot catch everything: names, health details or other personal information you type into a question can end up in the sample, so please do not enter anything you do not want stored. Memory snippets and profile data are not part of it, and it cannot be read from the app. Because these entries are not linked to any account, we usually cannot assign them to you and therefore cannot delete them on request.
  • Anonymous plan statistics: daily counters of plan events (for example how often the coach offered a plan, how often creating a plan failed, or how often a plan was not offered because it requires Premium) without any reference to a person.

7. AI Coaching & AI Features (EU-hosted models, EU-internal)

Processing pipeline: AI requests (coaching and plan generation) pass from a Supabase Edge Function in the EU to Eden AI (Eden AI SAS, France, EU), an AI-API aggregator, via its EU endpoint. Eden AI forwards them to EU-hosted language models: currently models of Mistral AI (Mistral AI SAS, France, EU). Only models on our list of EU-hosted models are used for these features. We may change the models or providers, and may connect to a provider's EU interface directly instead of going through Eden AI; in every case the processing for these features takes place on servers in the EU, and this page is updated accordingly. The entire pipeline runs inside the EU, so there is no third-country transfer for coaching or plan generation. OpenAI is no longer used.

Legal basis: Before AI Coaching is activated, you give explicit consent during onboarding to the processing of your health data for AI coaching purposes, including AI-generated training and meal plans (Section 4c) (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Because the pipeline is EU-internal, no separate consent to a third-country transfer is required. AI Coaching is not available unless this consent is given. You may withdraw it at any time by disabling AI Coaching in app settings; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Data sent to the AI provider per coaching request:

  • Your name (pseudonymized) — Your preferred name is not sent in plain text. Instead, Pulse uses a placeholder (%name%) that the AI model includes in its responses. The placeholder is replaced with your real name only on your device before the response is displayed to you. The AI provider never learns your actual name.
  • Your age range (e.g. 20–29) — never your exact age or date of birth
  • Your biological sex (from Apple Health or manual entry)
  • Your height and weight (from Apple Health or manual entry)
  • Your coaching question (the message you typed)
  • Relevant memory snippets — up to 5,000 characters of saved coaching context and notes stored in the app (also using the pseudonymized name placeholder)
  • Health analysis summaries — computed scores and trend analyses produced on your device (not raw sensor readings such as individual HRV samples)
  • Your coaching profile from onboarding — main goal, experience level, preferred coaching style, the optimization goal you typed and, if you entered them, illness notes and physical limitations (free text; each of these three free-text fields is limited to 500 characters)
  • If you gave the separate medication consent: the drug classes you declared (for example beta blocker or SSRI/SNRI) — never medication names, brands, doses or times

Safeguards:

  • Name pseudonymization: Your name is not included in data sent to the AI provider, which significantly reduces the risk of identification — though other data (biological sex, height, weight, and your coaching question) is still sent.
  • Data minimization: We do not send raw HealthKit sensor readings (e.g. individual heart-rate measurements) or your complete chat history.
  • Encryption in transit: All data is encrypted via TLS 1.3 during transmission.
  • EU relay: Requests pass through a Supabase Edge Function in the EU, serving as an intermediary layer before reaching Eden AI (France) and the EU-hosted models (Mistral AI, France).

What is NOT sent to the AI provider: Your email address, authentication credentials, raw HealthKit sensor readings (e.g. individual heart-rate measurements), or your complete chat history (the AI provider receives only a summary of your coaching history).

Location: 🇪🇺 European Union (Eden AI and Mistral AI in France)

Retention: Retention of AI request data (coaching and plan generation) is governed by the Eden AI and Mistral AI data-processing terms. We are finalizing the applicable data-processing agreements and will update this page once the retention periods are confirmed.

Protection & Transfer Mechanism: Because the AI pipeline is EU-internal (Eden AI in France forwarding to EU-hosted models of Mistral AI in France), no third-country transfer arises for coaching or plan generation. An Eden AI data-processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) and a due-diligence review of Mistral AI are being finalized before broad rollout (Eden AI DPA + Mistral AI due-diligence pending).

Opt-out: You can disable AI Coaching in the app settings at any time. Tracking of fitness, nutrition and sleep keeps working without AI Coaching; the coaching chat and AI-generated plans are then unavailable.
